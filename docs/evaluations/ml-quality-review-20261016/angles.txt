labels|TRAINING DATA AND LABELS. How feedback becomes training rows: up/down/neutral handling and weights, re-votes and dedup, class imbalance, recency/time decay of old votes (taste drift), exposure/selection bias (the model only sees votes on stories it chose to show), unused implicit signals (impressions without votes, dwell, article opens, comments_open), label noise. Check pipeline/ranking.py training-row construction, feedback.py, database queries feeding training.
features|MODEL AND FEATURES. The 394-d feature vector plus side embeddings (embeddinggemma), the RBF SVM (C, gamma; note config.toml svm_c vs docs), the linear blend (word/TF-IDF), score combination and min-max normalization, the tier blend/dual gate ramp, k-NN similarity features with LOOCV self-exclusion, positive k-means clusters, source one-hots, z-score clipping. Look for representation weaknesses, mis-scaling, combinations that wash out signal, calibration problems, and concrete cheap model improvements (e.g. ensembling, better text fields, pairwise/ranking losses) that the prior research did not already rule out.
eval|EVALUATION METHODOLOGY. Offline eval scripts in scripts/ and docs/evaluations: splits (temporal vs random, group isolation), leakage paths (similarity features, TF-IDF vocabulary fitted on test, dedup, re-votes), metrics used (AUC, NDCG, credited up-rate) and whether they measure the four aims, the interleaving trial's attribution and statistics (scripts/interleave_report.py, pipeline/interleave.py), power. Propose a better offline protocol and metric suite (including diversity/novelty/coverage metrics) that would let model changes be judged reliably on small data.
diversity|DIVERSITY, DISCOVERY AND EXPLORATION. How the ranked list is assembled from model scores: Recommended, Explore/Discover, far card, Unsure entropy, caps, dedup, selection passes (ARCHITECTURE 3.4-3.5, pipeline/ranking.py, pipeline/__init__.py). Does the model itself drive sameness (e.g. nearest-neighbour features rewarding near-duplicates of past ups)? Propose model-level or re-ranking changes (MMR/DPP-style diversification, topic-coverage constraints, calibrated exploration such as Thompson sampling on model uncertainty, novelty features) that keep hits while widening topics, and how to measure novel up-votes and topic spread offline.
coldstart|SHORT HISTORY, NEW PROFILES AND FRESHNESS. Behaviour for users below the dual gate (tiers 1-2, centroid-diff), the 20/80 ramp, warm-start, a new profile's first feed, and freshness/staying current (age handling, candidate windows, popularity priors, important developments the user would want regardless of taste). Propose improvements (priors from popularity or from the main profile's population, better tier-2 scorer, faster ramp, freshness/importance features) with offline validation (short-history curve replays).
code|CORRECTNESS OF THE RANKING CODE PATH. Hunt for bugs that silently degrade ranking quality: feature construction mismatches between training and inference, cache-key/signature staleness (model cache, warm store, candidate cache), scaler fitted on wrong rows, side-vector coverage/norm issues (pipeline/side_embeddings.py), missing-embedding handling, min-max with ties or single rows, NaN paths, interleaving arms sharing mutable state, source one-hot misclassification, off-by-one in selection margins. Trace pipeline/ranking.py and pipeline/linear_blend.py end to end. Each bug needs a concrete trigger scenario.
